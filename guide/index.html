EV Charging MVP — Build Guide

One server. Chargers talk OCPP 1.6J. Drivers use Telegram (no app). Admins use a Next.js dashboard.

Open one page per day. Do the checklist. Tick the boxes (they're saved in your browser). Don't move on until "Done when" passes.

What we're building

  • Charger connects to our server over WebSocket (/ocpp/CP_101).
  • Driver scans a QR on the charger → Telegram opens → bot says "Fleet Mode, Free" or "Public, ₹18/kWh" → driver taps ⚡ Start.
  • Tenant admin (fleet company / society) manages their chargers, members and tariffs in the dashboard.
  • Super admin (us) creates tenants and registers chargers.

The 3 rules that matter most

  1. Tenant isolation. A tenant admin can never see another tenant's data. Always use tenantDb(tenantId) in tenant routes.
  2. Access waterfall. Member of this charger's tenant with an allowed role → member tariff. Otherwise, if PUBLIC is allowed → public tariff + wallet check. Otherwise → reject.
  3. Lock the price. Copy mode and pricePerKwh onto the session when it starts. Bill from the session, never from the current tariff.
